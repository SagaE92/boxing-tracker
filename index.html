Why this is better than commercial trackers

  • Size first — designed to live inside hand wraps. Commercial units are usually larger.
  • Honest numbers — clipping and saturation are flagged. No silent “power” claims.
  • Open algorithm — detection rules and raw data are available. You can improve them.
  • Kickboxer-aware — hooks, spinning, body work, and low-confidence kick/clinch tags.
  • Tiny battery, real week — on-device detection + deep sleep targets ~10 sessions on 40–60 mAh.

Full comparison in docs/why.md.
